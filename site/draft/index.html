A skill you install once, anywhere.

Precise, direct, natural AI writing.

AI drafts arrive padded with filler, fake enthusiasm, contrast framing, and dashes, and a rewrite can bend the facts inside them. Clean Language is a small file of written rules you add to your AI once: it removes those habits and tells it to keep your facts, figures, names, quotations, and technical terms exactly as you wrote them. When a style rule and the meaning conflict, the meaning wins.

What it gives you

AI drafts arrive padded with filler and fake enthusiasm, and a rewrite can quietly change a figure. Clean Language is a small file of writing rules you add to your assistant once. From then on its drafts read as if a capable person wrote them, and your facts, figures, names, and quotations are kept as you gave them, by instruction. It works in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot, free to use under CC BY-SA 4.0. It improves the writing itself; getting past AI detectors is outside its aim.
